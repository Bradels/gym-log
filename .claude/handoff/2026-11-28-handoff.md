## Lessons from this week

- **Chicken breasts here are 500–600 g each** (1–1.2 kg per two), roughly double a typical
  breast and very thick. Cut the thin end off, then slice the thick end flat into 2–3 cutlets
  (~2 cm each). Trust the user's own log over general-knowledge sizes.
- **Peanut butter:** weigh it; 30 g is one serve (~190 kcal, 15 g fat). It has been the main
  reason fat ran over target most days.
- **Sugar:** "2 tablespoons" is ~25 g, about 6 teaspoons. One sugar ≈ one teaspoon ≈ 16 kcal.
- **Microwave:** a Kmart Anko, probably 700–900 W. Packet times are often written for
  1,000–1,100 W: multiply by ~1.4, then add 30 seconds at a time.
- **Go-to meals:** chicken parcel and tuna rice bowl (both pinned in `data/recipes.json`), the
  usual Subway footlong (Sweet Onion Chicken Teriyaki on malted rye, ~720 kcal), and Nando's
  with the swaps in Fri 27's notes (spicy rice instead of drumsticks, one Nata).
